Your buying process

Every purchase is different, but a well-managed process reduces surprises and protects your interests.

1

Plan

Clarify your goals, preferred areas, timing, financing, and must-have features.

2

Search

Review available homes, compare values, and identify properties worth seeing in person.

3

Offer

Structure an offer using current market conditions, comparable sales, and your comfort level.

4

Inspect

Coordinate due diligence and help you understand inspection findings and next steps.

5

Negotiate

Address price, repairs, credits, contingencies, and other terms strategically.

6

Close

Track deadlines, coordinate with all parties, and help guide the transaction to completion.
