Your buying process

Six steps, carefully managed.

Every purchase is different, but a well-managed process reduces surprises and protects your interests.

01

Plan

Clarify your goals, preferred areas, timing, financing, and must-have features.

02

Search

Review available homes, compare values, and identify properties worth seeing.

03

Offer

Structure an offer using market conditions, comparable sales, and your comfort level.

04

Inspect

Coordinate due diligence and help you understand findings and next steps.

05

Negotiate

Address price, repairs, credits, contingencies, and other terms strategically.

06

Close

Track deadlines, coordinate with all parties, and guide the transaction to completion.
